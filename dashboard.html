Blue measures delivery from the SSD-backed share to Plex, including data already in RAM and SMB overhead. It does not measure physical SSD reads or the iPad connection. Gaps mean no delivery measurement was available.

What this means

Start a film in Plex to see read-ahead activity here.

A burst means data is being fetched from the original media. A flat green line means the cache engine has not fetched more content during that sample. Blue can still show cached content being delivered to Plex. This does not describe unrelated disk activity or whether a disk is asleep.

Burst mode fills ahead to 999 MiB, then waits until about 250 MiB remains before refilling. Each file and playback region is checked independently; simultaneous films share the overall SSD budget. Cached copies can be reused; original files stay intact.
